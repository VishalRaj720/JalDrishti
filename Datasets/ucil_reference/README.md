# UCIL reference documents

Public documents of the Uranium Corporation of India Ltd, the operator of every
uranium mine in Jharkhand. They were downloaded on 2026-09-26 for the grounding
pass (`docs/LIMITATIONS.md` §1k), with the owner's permission. They are kept
here so every number taken from them can be checked against the page it came
from.

| File | What it is | Where it came from | Used for |
|---|---|---|---|
| `ucil_pfr_narwapahar_expansion_2017.pdf` | Pre-feasibility report, Narwapahar mine expansion to 0.6 MTPA (2017), 26 pp. | environmentclearance.nic.in (MoEFCC ToR application, 09 Oct 2017) | Mine discharge 2,700 m³/day; developed to 380 m; "material above the 100 mRL ... thoroughly oxidized"; ore specific gravity 2.8 |
| `ucil_pfr_turamdih_augmentation_2017.pdf` | Pre-feasibility report, Turamdih mine augmentation to 0.75 MTPA (2017), 16 pp. | environmentclearance.nic.in | Mine discharge ≥ 3,170 m³/day; working levels 60–230 m bgl, shaft bottom 260 m |
| `ucil_pfr_banduhurang_augmentation_2017.pdf` | Pre-feasibility report, Banduhurang open-cast augmentation to 1.5 MTPA (2017), 12 pp. | environmentclearance.nic.in | Pit water is Turamdih's dewatering; ridge topography; rainfall 1,391 mm |
| `ucil_observations_u_mineralisation_turamdih.pdf` | "Some observations on uranium mineralisation at Turamdih" | ucil.gov.in (feature article) | Economic ore from 50 m to 200 m; lodes 1.5–40 m thick, dip ~35° NE |
| `ucil_u_mineralisation_and_development_turamdih.pdf` | "U mineralisation & development at Turamdih" | Internet Archive copy of ucil.gov.in (2017-08-09) | Same 50–200 m statement |
| `ucil_uranium_mining_jharkhand_new_ventures_2004.pdf` | "Uranium mining in Jharkhand — new ventures", JHMIN-04 seminar, Dhanbad, 28 Feb 2004 | Internet Archive copy of ucil.gov.in (2017-03-29) | Jaduguda shaft 640 → 905 m; Bhatin winzes to 135 m; Banduhurang pit to 164 m; Bagjata lodes to 600 m |
| `ucil_radiological_impact_assessment_bagjata.pdf` | "Radiological impact assessment in Bagjata uranium deposit" | Internet Archive copy of ucil.gov.in (2017-09-27) | Reserves to 270 m; old levels at 60 and 100 m; lode to 600 m |
| `ucil_uranium_mining_by_in_situ_leaching.pdf` | "Uranium mining by in-situ leaching" | Internet Archive copy of ucil.gov.in (2017-09-27) | ISL preconditions, including "the host rock should have a good permeability" |
| `ucil_vein_type_u_mineralisation_jaduguda.pdf` | Sarangi & Singh, "Vein type uranium mineralisation in Jaduguda uranium deposit" (extended abstract) | Internet Archive copy of ucil.gov.in (2022-07-14) | Two lodes from the surface, 600 m along strike and 900 m down dip |

Journal papers used in the same pass are cited in
