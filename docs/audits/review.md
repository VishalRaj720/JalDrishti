**What moves each sub-score:** Physics +10 if migration is re-based on the down-gradient front and fractured retardation becomes species-dependent (both fixable with data already on disk); ML +6 if labels are re-baked after those fixes and radium's restoration law is unified train/serve; Architecture +5 if the species/residual constant tables collapse to one source; Frontend +4 if the radial-pin migration card shows `max_downgradient_m` instead of the radial number.

## 4. System Architecture & Pipeline Design

**Flow.** `Datasets/` (CGWB aquifer polygons, water quality, water levels; GSI lineaments; GLO-30 DEM; UCIL/UDEPO ore; HydroRIVERS; Texas ISR records; NAQUIM extracts) → `data_prep/` loaders build three gridded artifacts (flow, strike, river fields) plus point lookups → `physics/transport.py` (Domenico + dual-porosity clock + Tang envelope + E1 disc + restoration deficit wave + first-order U attenuation) → `synthetic/generate.py` (900 scenarios × 5 times × 4 species = 18,000 rows, 48-draw common-random-number MC labels) → `ml/train.py` (XGBoost per-band squared-error heads, P50-only monotone constraints, GroupKFold + leave-aquifer-out, Mondrian split-CQR with a held-out coverage gate) → `dashboard/server.py` + `resolve.py` (pin → hydrogeology, ore gating, D5/3.3 K corrections, envelope flags) → `frontend/ml_pipeline/` (vanilla JS + Leaflet; rendering only).

**Train == serve.** Mostly real: one feature builder (`build_feature_row`) is shared by generator and server; `kd_range_for()` centralizes Kd after a past radium KeyError; the training envelope is read from the *deployed* model card, not live config (survives config drift); the E1 flag is toggled with try/finally. Verified consistencies: identical `restoration_source_fraction`/`disc_flush_factor` composition at [transport.py:669-671](../../ml_pipeline/physics/transport.py:669) and [generate.py:294-296](../../ml_pipeline/synthetic/generate.py:294). **Verified divergence:** the radium restoration endpoint (Finding #3) — the exact class the project's history warns about, still alive because per-species residuals live in three places (Texas loader output, config fallback dict without a radium key, and `RADIUM_RESTORATION_RESIDUAL` consumed only by the generator).

**Analytical↔surrogate interaction.** Both engines run per request; disagreement is recorded in a rolling drift monitor (verified live: medians per metric, `drifting` gated on n≥20). Non-ore uranium and any future non-ML species bypass the surrogate with explicit status strings. Conformal deltas are Mondrian per regime×species; analytical P50s fell inside the ML P10–P90 in every probe I ran (area: 14.6 ∈ [11.3, 29.2]; sulfate 14.4 ∈ [5.9, 53.2]; radium 13.7 ∈ [5.2, 49.4]).

**Performance (measured).** Warm `/api/predict` (both engines + contours + MC excursion + vertical screening): **0.10–0.12 s**; cold first request 2.2 s (artifact load). Direct: analytical ~0.01 s, ML ~0.02 s warm, resolve ~0.05 s. The month-stepped timeline animation (one POST per frame, self-paced `await` loop in [app.js:398-410](../../frontend/ml_pipeline/app.js:398)) is comfortably within budget at its 2–24 fps settings; requests cannot pile up by construction. No performance findings.

## 5. Hydrogeological & Scientific Audit

Answers to each §7.1 question:

**1. Is Domenico defensible at all in fractured schist?** As a *screening* frame, yes — with one structural exception. The EPM + dual-porosity clock + matrix-diffusion envelope stack is the standard screening treatment (BIOSCREEN-class + Goltz–Roberts + Neretnieks), and the system's own documents state its limits (no discrete networks, no channeling, a smooth ellipse where reality is fingers). That is a fidelity limitation, honestly disclosed, not a category error — *for weakly sorbing solutes*. The category error is narrower and real: **front kinematics carry no species chemistry in the fractured regime** (Finding #2). For U/SO₄/TDS with Kd ≤ 3 the error is modest (matrix sorption capacity β·R_m vs β: factor ~90 at Kd=1 — already material for uranium late-time behaviour, though partially inside the β band's intent); for Ra-226 it is 4–5 orders of magnitude and invalidates the radium product in the regime it is actually served. Domenico survives; the fractured retardation wiring does not.

**2. Tang kernel as early-arrival envelope (`max`)?** Not double-counting — `max()` selects one solution, never sums. The composition is a deliberate conservative union and behaves as documented for weakly sorbing species: I verified σ(Kd) ordering and that Tang reach responds to Kd (σ = 5.1 at Kd=1 vs 113 at Kd=500). Two caveats: (a) referencing the Tang time scaling to a containment-held water front (`t_w = (x/Xw)·t` with Xw from the η-held `front_position`) is a heuristic outside the Tang solution's assumptions (constant-flux steady fracture flow) — acceptable for screening, unlabelled; (b) because the envelope can only *extend*, the *lower* branch (the species-blind continuum front) controls the error for sorbing species — the `max` structure is only as honest as its weaker member (see Q1).

**3. Containment η = min(1, Q_net/(q·b·W))?** The right screening abstraction — it is exactly the regulatory sizing argument for a bleed — but optimistic in fractured rock, where capture is network-topology-dependent and individual transmissive channels can bypass an on-paper-complete capture. η = 1.0 is reached at Jaduguda defaults (2% bleed vs tiny regional throughflow). The MC's Q_net drift (×0.6–1.3) and downtime degradation partially represent this; a fractured-specific η penalty (or wider drift) would be more honest. Defensible with a caveat that is currently implicit only.

**4. E1 disc / radial geometry and anisotropy-from-V?** The disc-as-union design is sound and its area accounting is correct (disc excluded from travel metrics — verified in code and by the disc_mask path). The V→α_T/α_L mapping (`0.02·exp((V−0.63)/0.20)` clipped [0.01, 0.10]) is an invented anchor — no citation exists for the functional form — but it is calibrated to reproduce the literature default at the field median, bounded within Gelhar's range, and documented as an anchoring rather than a measurement; acceptable. The display-only flux rotation (λ = 1+5(1−V), capped 6) is a reasonable heuristic and correctly quarantined from labels. **However, the E1 grid extension (upstream to cover the disc) is what exposed the artifact box's corner to the migration metric** — Finding #1 is an E1-era regression the E1 design doc does not anticipate.

**5. K(z) exponential depth decay per district?** Directionally correct and well-evidenced — exponential K decline under lithostatic closure is standard for crystalline rock, and calibrating λ to each district's NAQUIM fracture-death depth is a genuinely good use of data on disk; clamping into the trained K box is the right serve-time discipline. Two defects: the district λ creates hard spatial steps (Finding #5), and a single deterministic λ per district carries no uncertainty into the bands (the decay factor at 180 m is 0.15 — a first-order multiplier served as exact).

**6. Vertical seasonal band: is Δi = Δh/dz across ~110 m valid? Wet-season floor at zero?** The quasi-steady gradient perturbation is defensible: with K_v ~ 0.011 m/d and fractured-rock S_s ~ 10⁻⁶–10⁻⁵ m⁻¹, hydraulic diffusivity is ~10³–10⁴ m²/day and the annual pressure signal penetrates ≥ the 110–140 m separation, so the head profile tracks the boundary condition within a season — a transient leaky-aquifer solution would refine, not overturn, the end members. The zero floor on the wet-season gradient is correct (a reversed gradient closes the upward pathway; it must not mint negative risk). The two-end-member band itself is the honest structure (trap 6 acknowledged — the band is the intent, and the in-phase bound is regression-locked). The remaining defect is presentational: per-season steady-state breakthrough times shown as arrival forecasts (Finding #8) — over decades the annual mean governs, and the annual mean is approximately the baseline row.

**7. Kd→Rd, matrix R_m, radium credibility in both regimes?** The porous chain is textbook-correct: Rd = 1 + ρ_b·Kd/n with L/kg→m³/kg conversion (verified: Ra porous Rd = 14,841; front collapses to 0.0 m). R_m inside σ is correctly formed ([transport.py:179](../../ml_pipeline/physics/transport.py:179)). The refusal to apply bulk-density retardation in fractured rock is correct as far as it goes — but the replacement (bare 1+β) omits sorption-scaled matrix capacity entirely, which is where fractured-rock retardation of strongly sorbing nuclides actually lives (Neretnieks; every crystalline-repository safety case). **Radium: porous credible, fractured wrong by ~10⁴–10⁵ ×** (Finding #2). The radium restoration residual derivation (exp(−N/Rd), anchored on uranium's measured 0.066 endpoint) is a defensible order-of-magnitude argument and I do not challenge it — the defect is that the serve path never uses it (Finding #3).

**8. Monsoon "shape from state, amplitude from pin"?** Defensible. One monsoon system over ~200 km; onset timing varies by days against a monthly interpolation grid, while amplitude genuinely varies per station (p10 2.17 → p90 6.19 m) — putting timing state-wide and amplitude per-pin allocates the evidence correctly, and the four-campaign linear interpolation honestly reproduces the fast-recovery/slow-recession asymmetry without inventing a rainfall model. The measured-vs-interpolated month labelling (8 of 12 months interpolated) is exactly the right disclosure.

**9. Species thresholds and incremental-vs-absolute convention?** Correct. U 30 ppb (WHO provisional, BIS-aligned), SO₄ 400 / TDS 2000 (BIS permissible — the legal breach values), Ra-226 **1000 mBq/L (WHO GDWQ)** with BIS's silence and AERB's deferral correctly documented. The incremental convention (`thr_inc = max(threshold − background, 0.1·threshold)`) is applied consistently across `plume_metrics`, the MC excursion, the vertical screening's concentration gate, and the generator (verified in all four call sites); reported concentrations are absolute. No inconsistency found.

**Strengths** (once, per the rules): the data skeleton is genuinely local (plane-fit flow field from real stations, real lineament fabric, real ore geography and grades, real Texas restoration anchors); the incremental-exceedance logic prevents blaming mining for natural background; the uncertainty machinery (envelope flags from the deployed card, held-out conformal coverage, drift monitor) is unusually disciplined for a screening tool.

**Gaps & simplifications:** no transient flow; first-order infinite-sink attenuation; homogeneity per polygon; 2-D + decoupled vertical; single-season steady breakthrough times; unsampled aperture/De; η optimistic in fractured rock — all disclosed except the last three.

**Category errors:** (1) species-blind fractured front kinematics — invalidates Ra-226 (and erodes late-time U) in the served regime; (2) the migration metric's upstream-box readout — not a physics error in the field solution, but it converts a documented artifact zone into the headline number, which has the same effect on users.
